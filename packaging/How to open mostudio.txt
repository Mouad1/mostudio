How to open mostudio (first time only)

The bundled video engine requires macOS 12 or later.

Find your ZIP

GitHub Releases > Assets: https://github.com/Mouad1/mostudio/releases
Choose the ZIP ending in -mac-arm64.zip. It runs on Apple Silicon Macs
(M1 or newer). On an Intel Mac, run mostudio from source (see README).

Downloaded ZIP -> Finder > Downloads -> mostudio folder -> Start mostudio
Locally built ZIP -> checkout > dist -> mostudio folder -> Start mostudio

1. In Finder, open Downloads for a browser download. If you saved it elsewhere,
   use "Show in Finder" in your browser's downloads list. Open "dist" for
   a locally built ZIP. Double-click the ZIP to extract the "mostudio" folder.
   If Finder already shows that folder, open it directly.
2. Open the folder and double-click "Start mostudio".
3. macOS says it can't check the app for malware. Click "Done" (not "Move to Trash").
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security. Scroll down to the message about
   "Start mostudio" and click "Open Anyway". Confirm with your password or Touch ID.
5. Double-click "Start mostudio" again and click "Open".
   A small Terminal window opens and your browser shows mostudio.

Every next time: just double-click "Start mostudio".
Keep the Terminal window open while you work. Close it to quit mostudio.

This build is unsigned. Only use Open Anyway for a ZIP from a source you trust.
The dashboard runs on your Mac. If you configure a cloud AI provider, it
receives the audio or text needed for the steps you run.

To update, extract the new ZIP and replace the old "mostudio" app folder.
Keep your workspace separate from the app folder. The default workspace is
"Movies/mostudio" in your home folder. App settings are saved in "~/.mostudio".
